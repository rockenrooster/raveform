# RAVEFORM — Look Bible (v1)

Creative direction for the renderer and effects. It complements `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §11 and
`docs/TECH_SPEC.md` §5: those say what exists and how it is built; this says how it should look, with
the numbers that were tuned in the offline look-dev tool (`design/tools/lookdev.py`).

Reference images, regenerated by the scripts in `design/tools/`:

| Image | Shows |
|---|---|
| `design/previews/palettes.png` | A mock frame in each of the six Track palettes |
| `design/previews/glyphs_1.png`, `glyphs_2.png` | Every enemy family at tiers I–V, plus variant marks, at sheet size and at in-game size |
| `design/previews/glyphs_player.png` | Player ship, halo-as-health, Shards, enemy bullets |
| `design/previews/frame_reef.png` | **The target frame:** minute ten at 2400×1080 with HUD and touch controls (`design/tools/frame_mock.py`) |

Data for the engine: `design/palettes.json`, `design/glyphs.json`, `design/vector_font.txt`.
The preview images are the target. If the engine's output differs visibly from them at the same
intensities, the engine is wrong before the numbers are.

## 1. Principles

1. **Light on black.** The world is made of thin luminous lines. Nothing is filled. Black space is part
   of the composition — keep at least half the screen dark outside Drops.
2. **Hot cores, coloured halos.** A line is a 1.5–2 px white-hot core whose colour lives in its bloom.
   Never make something brighter by making it fatter.
3. **Everything breathes with the music**, a little all the time and a lot on the Drop.
4. **Threat reads first.** Danger has its own colour, its own shape and its own layer.
5. **Shape says who, detail says how dangerous, colour says which side.**

## 2. Colour

Palettes are authored in OKLCH and exported as linear-light sRGB (`palettes.json`, field `linear`).
Multiply by the light level from §3 to get the HDR value.

Constants (identical in every palette):

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Danger | `#fd3f5c` hot pink-red | Enemy bullets, lasers, lethal trails, telegraphs — and nothing else, ever |
| P1 / P2 / P3 / P4 | `#30ecf5` cyan / `#9bf52c` lime / `#b47efa` violet / `#fdca38` gold | Ship, halo, shots, that player's Shards and UI |
| Pickup | `#fdefba` white-gold | Shards, Boost orbs, Trio orbs |
| White | `#ffffff` | Core lines, flashes, neutral UI |

Per-palette roles: `bg0` (edge of the background), `bg1` (centre glow), `grid` (lattice at rest),
`gridHot` (lattice when stretched — the grid hue pushed almost to white, never a saturated colour, so
a hot lattice cannot be mistaken for an enemy), `accent` (titles, Drop flash, palette-coloured UI), and five
`enemyCandidates` of which three are used per run. The selection rule is stored in the JSON
(`selectionRule`): skip candidates within 30° of the danger hue or 25° of a present player's hue.

Enemy hue assignment: the three hues are dealt to families by role — hue 1 to the Track's fodder
families, hue 2 to its hunters and heavies, hue 3 to its specials — so colour hints at behaviour while
silhouette identifies the family.

Background: `bg0`/`bg1` at 55% as a soft radial gradient centred slightly off the player. It must never
exceed 0.12 luminance; the scene, not the backdrop, makes the picture.

## 3. Light levels

HDR intensity multipliers on the linear colour. Widths are reference pixels (1 = screen height / 1080).

| Element | Intensity | Width | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grid at rest | 0.30 | 1.1 | Colour `grid` |
| Grid stretched | warm 0.42, hot 0.85 → 1.2 | 1.15–1.3 | Graded with displacement: most of a disturbed region is only warm (40% toward `gridHot`); just the shock front itself goes hot. This is what makes explosions feel physical without turning the screen into mesh |
| Enemy hull (layer 0) | 1.8 at tier I → 2.6 at tier V | 1.7 | |
| Enemy inner ornament (layer 1) | hull × 0.72 | 1.45 | Counter-rotates; steps on hats |
| Enemy core mark (layer 2) | hull × 1.15 | 1.45 | |
| Variant marks B and C | 1.8, white | 1.5 | Drawn in white so they read as equipment, not body |
| Elite | hull + 0.6, plus a thin white copy of the hull at 1.0 | | Elites always have a white core line |
| Player hull | 2.6 in player colour, plus a white copy at 2.0 | 2.1, white copy 0.8 | The only two-tone hull in the game |
| Player halo | 1.3 | 1.4 | Rotates slowly; pulses on kicks |
| Player shots | 4.5, colour mixed 55% toward white | 2.0 | Capsules 13–18 wu long |
| Riff effects | 3.0–4.0 | 1.6–2.4 | |
| Enemy bullets | 2.4 in the scene **and** redrawn on the threat layer | 2.0 | Threat layer: 1.9 px line with a 1.3 px dark rim |
| Shards | 3.0 | 1.2 | |
| Sparks at birth | 2.0–5.5, fading with exponent 1.6 | 1.3 | Start 35% toward white |
| Shock rings | 0.9–1.2 | 1.2 | White |
| World text, HUD | 0.9–1.8 | 0.55 × glyph unit | |

Bloom (the preview's settings, to be matched): six scales from finest to coarsest with weights
0.26, 0.22, 0.18, 0.14, 0.11, 0.09, total strength 0.9. The coarsest scale is about a tenth of the
screen height. Low tiers drop the coarsest scales and renormalise.

Tone map (constants proven in the previews):

```
m     = max(r, g, b)                       // after exposure
tm    = min(1, m * (1 + m / 36) / (1 + m)) // extended Reinhard, white point 6
rgb   = rgb * tm / m                       // hue-preserving
t     = smoothstep(1, 8, m) * 0.7          // very hot values bleed toward white
rgb   = mix(rgb, vec3(tm), t)
```

## 4. Shapes

`glyphs.json` holds every shape as line segments in unit space, +x forward, each tagged with a layer:
0 hull, 1 inner ornament, 2 core mark. `size` is the visual radius relative to a standard enemy
(14 wu). `facesHeading` says whether the shape turns to face its motion or spins freely.

- **Silhouette is the family, and never changes with tier.** Hexagon, arrowhead, crescent, kite,
  horned wedge, petal ring, dart, serpent, nested squares, sharp star, gear turret, egg sac, triangle
  node, trefoil, broken rings, figure eight.
- **Tier adds layers and edges**, never a new outline.
- **Variant marks are shared equipment drawn in white:** B (ranged) is twin barrels beside the nose on
  heading shapes (`mark_b_heading`) or three outward spikes on spinning shapes (`mark_b_spin`);
  C (special) is a pair of bracket arcs (`mark_c`). Variant A has no mark.
- **Low-detail LOD:** drop layers 1 and 2.
- **Motion:** spinning families rotate layer 0 slowly (quarter turn per bar) and layer 1 the other way
  at twice the rate; layer 1 snaps one step on each hat. All enemies swell 5–8% on the kick.

Traits (elite auras), drawn by the effect system on top of the base glyph:

| Trait | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Armored | Second hull outline at 112% scale, dim |
| Swift | Three short speed streaks behind |
| Giant | Scale × 1.8 |
| Splitting | A crack line through the hull that widens with damage |
| Volatile | Hull flickers toward the danger colour; pulsing core dot |
| Regenerating | Small plus sign orbiting |
| Cloaked | Drawn at 8% between beats, full on the beat flash |
| Shielded | Thick white frontal arc |
| Magnetic | Two horseshoe arcs; stolen Shards orbit it |
| Twin | Thin tether line in the enemy hue |
| Phasing | Every other hull segment hidden off the beat |
| Commander | Three crown ticks and a faint aura ring |

### The player

The ship is a swept dart with a notched tail and a diamond core (`ship`). It is the only two-tone
object in the game (player colour plus a white copy), it always leaves a trail, and it carries a
**halo: one arc per Shield cell** (`halo_1` … `halo_6`). Health is read off the ship, not the HUD.
Losing a cell shatters one arc outward; the last arc pulses in the danger colour.

## 5. Motion and effects

Explosion recipes by size (counts are for the High tier; scale by the governor's density):

| Kill | Sparks | Speed (wu/s) | Life (s) | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small (tier I–II) | 14–20 | 180–520 | 0.35–0.7 | One ring to 48 wu in 0.18 s; grid impulse 60 |
| Medium (tier III–IV) | 36–48 | 200–640 | 0.4–0.9 | Two rings; six slow embers living 1.2 s; grid impulse 140; shake 1.5 px |
| Large (tier V, elite) | 90–120 in a fast thin band and a slow thick band | 150–900 | 0.4–1.2 | Three rings; **shatter** — the enemy's own segments fly apart as tumbling capsules for 0.5 s; grid impulse 300; distortion ring; shake 3 px |
| Boss phase break | 300+ | up to 1200 | up to 1.6 | Clamped white flash; large distortion; shake 6 px; 50 ms hit-stop |

Spark drag 3.2 /s; streak length = speed × 0.035 s, capped at 26 wu; colour starts 35% toward white and
cools to the enemy hue.

Other signature effects:

- **Shot impact (no kill):** 3–4 sparks back along the shot, 0.15 s.
- **Spawn warp-in:** a ring in the enemy hue contracting from 90 wu to 0 over exactly two beats, then a
  flash on the beat as the enemy becomes solid.
- **Telegraphs (danger colour):** lasers show a thin dashed line for their charge time, then the full
  beam; dashes show chevrons along the path; shooters glint one beat before firing.
- **Nova:** on press, sparks appear on a 600 wu ring and fall inward while the screen dims; on the beat
  a thick white front expands to 1400 wu in 0.35 s with 400 radial sparks and a bloom pump (+60% for
  120 ms; +20% in reduce-flashing mode).
- **Dash:** five fading ghost copies of the ship; a Perfect Dash adds a ring and a four-point sparkle.
- **Shield cell lost:** the arc shatters, a panic ring pushes outward, time dips to 85% for 200 ms.
- **Afterglow:** everything leaves a short phosphor trail (time constant about 110 ms).

Beat response amounts are in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §11.1.

## 6. The target frame and the HUD

`design/previews/frame_reef.png` is what milestone M1's stress scene and M3's HUD should resemble.
Lessons from composing it, which are now rules:

1. **Rings are a language.** White shock rings belong to explosions and last under 0.2 s — only the
   newest explosion shows one. Rings made by a player's Riffs use that player's colour. Interface rings
   (the sticks) are dashed. Three kinds of circle that looked alike made the first draft unreadable.
2. **Keep the lattice quiet.** See the grid rows in §3.
3. **The player must win the eye.** Two-tone hull, halo, trail and the player-coloured Riff effects
   together make the ship findable in under a second at this density. Do not add white effects near it.

HUD layout at 2400×1080 (scale with screen height; keep a 132 px side margin clear of the camera cut-out):

| Element | Place | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Score | Top left, cap height 34 | White, 1.5 |
| Chain | Under the score, cap height 22 | Gold, pulses on pickup |
| Track and tempo | Top centre, cap height 12 | Palette accent |
| **Phrase bar** | Top centre under it, 520 px wide | Four segments sized by bars (Groove, Build, Drop, Break), the current one lit in the accent colour, a white playhead, and "DROP IN n" in bars — the player can always see the song's shape |
| Nova stock | Top right | One four-point star per Nova, lit in the player colour |
| Pause | Top right corner | 28 px ring with two bars |
| Riff slots | Bottom centre | Four hexagons with five level pips each |
| Active Boosts | Above the Riff slots | Name plus one pip per bar remaining |
| Health | On the ship | The halo (§4) — there is no health bar |
| Sticks | Bottom corners | Dashed 150 px rings at 0.34, a filled thumb pad that follows the thumb |
| Nova and Dash buttons | Above and inside the aim stick | Idle: outline at 0.5 with a label. **Pressed: filled disc in the player colour, outline at 2.0 and thicker, label at 1.8** |
| Edge indicators | Screen edges | Small chevrons: white for elites, gold for events, danger colour for incoming ranged attacks |

No world object spawns under the score, phrase bar or Nova stock.
