# RAVEFORM — final creative, design and FX review (2026-11-15)

Findings only: nothing in the game was changed by this review (the owner: "no fixing just findings").
Build reviewed: `e5dccdb` (Fusions shown, a Nova a Shard level, the count-in 3-2-1).

## How it was done

- **Pictures.** About 1,030 fresh pictures of the game, rendered on the PC off screen: every screen (phone shape
  2400x1080 and the tablet's 1920x1200), the Trio and the HUD, every upgrade's scene, every fight's phases and death, the
  arena's structures and events, the elites, the enemy families and their glyph sheets, every Track's first Drop, the
  bot's real run from minute 7 to minute 23 at effect tiers low (the Moto), high and ultra (the tablet), the feedback
  strips, the title and the transitions, every co-op screen and moment. They are in `captures\final\` (ignored by git;
  contact sheets in `captures\final\sheets\`, working notes in `captures\final\notes.md`).
- **Measurements.** Brightness of frames with `design/tools/scenes.py light`; 20 simulated runs of 30 minutes
  (`--sim crowd --trace`, the careful picker on the assist's fire, and the novice with hits costing nothing) for which
  phases a fight reaches, how long it lasts and what it costs.
- **Design against code.** Seven reviewers (Opus) each read one area's design pages against its code: screens and HUD,
  look and FX, upgrades, fights of Tracks 1-5, fights of Tracks 6-10, arena / elites / enemies, sound cues and haptics.
  They ran nothing. Their full notes are in `captures\final\audit\*.md`.
- **What each finding rests on** is marked: **[seen]** in a picture, **[measured]**, **[checked]** = I confirmed it in
  the code myself, **[reported]** = a reviewer's finding I have not re-checked.
- **Not done:** nothing was listened to (PC runs are silent) and nothing was looked at on a phone or the tablet.

## Verdict

The game has a strong, consistent identity and it holds across all ten Tracks: the ten palettes are distinct, the sixteen
enemy families read by silhouette, every boss looks like nothing else in the game, and the rule "what can hurt is red,
with a glow and a dark rim" is kept almost everywhere, on the Moto's low tier too. The Trio, the title, the transitions
and the co-op screens are clean. The feedback effects added on 2026-11-14 (crits, Crescendo, the Vault, the meteors, the
bosses' deaths) are the best-looking part of the game.

What is wrong falls in five groups: (1) late in a run the grid's own glow takes over the screen; (2) every screen is laid
out for one screen shape; (3) a handful of upgrades and rules do not do what the player is told; (4) several attacks and
events can hurt without the warning the design gives them; (5) the game's biggest moments (SET COMPLETE, the loop's new
rules) are its least readable.

## High

**H1. Late in a run the grid glows so much the ship is lost; worse on the tablet than on the Moto.** [seen] [measured] [checked]
- look.md 3 sets the busiest frame at mean luminance 0.148 with "just the shock front itself" hot. Single frames of the
  bot's run (high tier; a frame's brightness depends on its moment, the trend is what counts). With hits, retrying after
  each death: minute 10 0.081, minute 15 0.132. With hits costing nothing, so the build keeps growing: minute 7 0.164,
  minute 11 0.175, minute 17 (Track 10) 0.142, minute 20 (the loop) 0.192, minute 23 0.225. Track 10's Drop with every
  upgrade maxed: 0.264 with 2.4% of the screen dark. Track 8's Drop is at 0.146 with a *starting* build, the lower two
  thirds bright green mesh.
- Same frames by tier: minute 17 low 0.116 / high 0.142 / ultra 0.146; minute 23 low 0.198 / high 0.225; the maxed Drop
  low 0.214 / high 0.264. The tablet has it a fifth to a quarter worse than the Moto.
- Cause: a kill pushes the grid 60 / 140 / 300 by size, dimmed only by the crowd gain (`play_view.cpp:447`), which follows
  the enemies *alive* in view (`play_view.cpp:954`). A build that kills on arrival keeps that count low, so 70-95 kills a
  second push at full strength. Nothing caps pushes a second or the hot area (`lattice.cpp`: the heat is measured, never
  fed back). Every tier-V death is a "large" kill (`species.h:101`) and Track 8 makes tier V the crowd
  (`species.json:146-148`); the design assumed about one large kill a second. The Drop's wave pushes 600 / 400 with no
  dimming (`play_view_b.cpp:105`). The grid's springs ignore the grid's spacing (`lattice.cpp:11`), and the tiers use 32 /
  24 / 20 / 16 wu, so the finer grid moves further and draws more lines.
- Smallest change: a kill budget beside the light budget (dim the pushes by the kills a second, as the crowd gain dims by
  the bodies); push a non-elite tier-V death as a medium kill on the grid only; put the Drop's wave on the crowd gain.

**H2. Every screen is laid out for the 20:9 phone; on the tablet controls leave the screen.** [seen] [reported: the cause]
- 1920x1200: SETTINGS, SOUND: the three sound cards, the MASTER and EFFECTS faders and the co-op switch run off both
  edges, DONE sits on HAPTICS. LOOK: CREDITS sits on 90%, DONE on 130%. Co-op lobby: seats 1 and 4 are cut by the edges.
  Join list: a long room name runs into the seat count ("LIVING ROOM4/4").
- Cause: the scale comes from the height only and rows sit on a 2,222-wide canvas round the centre (`screens.cpp:62`,
  `play_hud.cpp:108`). Also reported for other shapes: on 16:9 DONE sits on 130%; on 4:3 the title's CO-OP and SETTINGS
  leave the screen; a ten-digit score on the game over is wider than any screen; with five or more Novas the stars run
  into "DROP IN n".
- Smallest change: scale by the smaller of height / 1080 and width / 2222.

**H3. On the auto-aim assist with Double-Time 1, Syncopation and Rear Guard do nothing.** [checked]
- The assist fires on every other step of the fire grid (`world.cpp:337`). At Double-Time 1 the grid is 16 ticks, so the
  assist's shots fall on ticks 0, 32 and 64 of a beat and never on 48, the off-beat both Mods need
  (`world_upgrades_m5.cpp:36`, `:348`, `:499`). Four picks that show nothing, for the way the owner plays, from the first
  Trio (which offers Double-Time) until Double-Time 2; they work at Double-Time 0 and 2, in a Surge and under Hyper.
  Reported: Rear Guard's counter-melody still plays.
- Smallest change: the assist never skips the off-beat step.

**H4. One Nova is worth a boss's phase, or the whole fight.** [checked: the rule] [reported: the numbers]
- A Nova does a flat 60 to every part of a boss it can hurt (`tuning.h:284`, `world_boss.cpp:651-668`), a number chosen
  against the bosses' page health. Their health has since been cut to 0.09-0.6 of the pages (`tuning.h:409-412`).
  Reported: a Nova with Longwave's head in view kills it (60 against 48.5), any Nova deletes every Longwave segment in
  view, three Novas take all of Kaleidos's arms or the whole Choir.
- Since today every Shard level also gives a Nova, and Auto Nova is on by default, so more of them land.
- Smallest change: scale the Nova's boss damage by the same health factor.

**H5. Feedback: a Nova comes back as a Null with no warning, and Auto Nova feeds it.** [checked: recorded and replayed] [reported: the rest]
- Every Nova is recorded and replayed by each Echo as a ring that grows like a Nova's front and hurts
  (`world_feedback.cpp:280`, `:359-367`, `:61-66`). Reported: the tape draws only the primary shots, so nothing shows it
  coming; Auto Nova fires at the last cell, its Null returns a bar or two later, Auto Nova fires again, until the stock is
  gone. The careful bot takes 3.5 hits in this fight against 0.2-0.9 in the first four.
- Smallest change: do not record Auto Nova's Novas; mark a Nova on the tape; show the Null's ring a beat before it grows.

**H6. Co-op: SYNC, opened from the shared pause, drops the phone from the room.** [checked]
- SETTINGS is offered on the co-op pause and SYNC is always offered there (`screens.cpp:837`). SYNC switches the app to
  the sync screen (`app.cpp:916-920`), where the game's frame, and with it the network session, no longer runs (it is
  called only while the game is the content: `app.cpp:853-857`, `:889-892`); the sync screen also restarts the band's
  song. After the link's timeout the phone is dropped; if it is the host, the room ends.
- Also: a player in SETTINGS or CREDITS when someone else resumes is put straight into the fight when the count ends
  (`play_coop.cpp:332-335`); the count-in is drawn only on the pause screen.
- Smallest change: SYNC disabled in co-op; a resume brings every device back to the pause screen for the count.

**H7. From Track 2 on a Vault, a Vault Drop, a Comet and a Convoy have several mini-bosses' worth of health.** [checked: the rule] [reported: the numbers]
- arena.md: "the mini-boss health of that Track times the fraction given". The arena uses the page's raw number
  (`world_arena.cpp:40-49`); the mini-bosses themselves use that number times their tune of 0.105-0.45
  (`world_boss.cpp:146-150`). Reported: Track 2's Vault 198 against Alpha Hound's 128 and the Hive's 69; a Comet crosses
  the frame in 1-2 s with 60 to 410.
- Smallest change: the same factor as the Track's mini-bosses.

**H8. A meteor, the Storm Cell and a Volatile blast hurt a ship sheltered in a level-up orb.** [checked: the meteor] [reported: the other two]
- GAME_DESIGN 6.1: "a ship inside an orb's ring cannot be hurt". Bodies, bullets and the bosses check it
  (`world.cpp:712`, `world_boss.cpp:1144`); the meteor's landing does not (`world_arena.cpp:447-451`). Taking an orb means
  standing still for two beats, which is a meteor's warning time, and Meteor Rain aims at the player.
- Smallest change: check the shelter once, where a hit is taken.

**H9. Cadence: in the Modulation the four pillars sweep round in one beat with no warning.** [checked: the timing] [reported: no picture of it]
- The turn starts on the bar line and its sound is sent on that same tick (`world_cadence.cpp:111-119`); it is a
  reversed cymbal that must end on its tick, so it plays nothing. Reported: nothing is drawn before the turn and the
  pillars hurt on contact at about 2,000 wu/s.
- Smallest change: send the cue a bar early and draw the pillars' arcs dashed over that bar.

**H10. In Track 6 the crowd is the colour of the pickups.** [checked: the data] [seen]
- Shards, Boost orbs and Cells are `#fdefba` (OKLCH 0.95 / 0.07 / 95); Zenith's fodder is `#fbefc2` (0.95 / 0.06 / 95)
  (`palettes.json:189-200`, `:1771-1783`). Reported: Furnace's hunters, Afterglow's and Mirage's fodder pass through the
  same gold. In the pictures of Track 6 the crowd, the Shards and the orbs are one colour; Tracks 5 and 7 (amber and gold
  crowds) are close.
- Smallest change (palette data): a gap round the pickup hue in the rule that picks enemy hues.

## Medium

### Look and FX

- **M1. A boss's or mini-boss's death turns the whole grid white, edge to edge.** [seen] Hive, Ram, Sentry Array,
  Arpeggiator, Cadence, Deep Well, Moth Queen. It is designed ("A boss falls") but it is the same mesh as H1 and it hides
  the ship while the crowd is still alive. Meteor Rain does the same over half the screen.
- **M2. SET COMPLETE, the end of the set, is the least readable moment in the game.** [seen] [reported] Thin gold text,
  cap 44 (the Track card it replaces is cap 110 with a write-on and a flare), drawn while the white and gold burst and
  500 Shards are on top of it; then about six bars of nothing before the loop's card.
- **M3. The loop's rules change cannot be read.** [seen] `ENCORE / TRACK 11 / FIRST LIGHT / NO MORE REFILLS / HALO 3`
  is shown for about four seconds with the full crowd already on screen and the ship on top of the ENCORE line; the two
  lines that change the game are small white text.
- **M4. Reduce flashing leaves on what it promises to remove.** [checked: shake] [reported: the rest] GAME_DESIGN 11.4:
  "disables strobing ... halves bloom pump and shake". Shake has no reduce-flashing factor (`play_view.cpp:942`).
  Reported: a boss's weak point under fire strobes white at 8-16 Hz, only dimmed; the Nova's white front, Black Hole's
  and Supernova's white pool, the Drop's wave and rings stay.
- **M5. A hit's rings, a revive's and a boss's fall vanish in a crowd.** [checked] They share the six slots meant for
  kill rings, the oldest thrown out (`play_view.cpp:318-339`); at 30 kills a second a ring lives a tenth of a second.
- **M6. The Drop's bloom burst never shows.** [checked] It is set on the Drop (`play_view_b.cpp:107`) and reset before it
  is used (`play_view.cpp:1567`, used at `:1705`).
- **M7. Frozen enemies do not look frozen.** [reported] [seen: no ice visible in a crowd] They keep spinning and swelling
  on the kick; the only mark is a 1 px ice-blue ring, next to the player's own colour.
- **M8. A Skitter's mine does not look like it hurts.** [reported] A 0.8 px ring with no rim or core, weaker than a tell.
- **M9. Co-op colours.** [reported] Every "gold" in the view is player 4's colour, so in a four-player run another
  player's Colossus, Cells and Boost orbs are drawn in P4's colour; and the enemy hues ignore who is playing (the
  four-player lists in the palettes are never read).
- **M10. A tell under the player's own light is lost.** [reported] On-Beat Crit's pools and stars and Storm Coil's glow
  are not merged or dimmed; five lanes on one weak point stack about 4.5 of gold over tells drawn at 0.35-0.6.

## What looks right

- The Moto's low tier keeps every signal: bullets keep their glow and core, tells, Cells, chevrons and lethal lines are
  on layers low does not cut. It is also the tier with the least glare.
- Lethal things are red with a glow in every fight looked at; bosses and mini-bosses are distinct at a glance.
- The Fusion's gold orb and the recipe lines read in real play (Seeker `+ SWARM 3/5 = MURMURATION`, Wave
  `+ SPLIT 4/4 = HELIX ARRAY` in the bot's run).
- The first-run hints are plain words. The 3-2-1 shows in solo and in co-op.
- Co-op on the phone's shape: the local network notice, the join screen in every state, the lobby, the beacon on and
  off screen, the revive, the shared pause, the game over: no validation messages, no controls closer than 24 px.
- The loop's card is built and does say ENCORE and NO MORE REFILLS; the halo burn happens.
- Reduce flashing is honoured by every screen and by the HUD.
- Nova's rules match GAME_DESIGN 5. No boss phase can stall. All sixteen families are built and in a roster.
